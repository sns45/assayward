# Self-demonstrating dogfood loop (§10)

assayward gates the releases of its sibling tools using their own real outputs:

- **forgeseal** produces the supply-chain attestations (CycloneDX SBOM, SLSA provenance, OpenVEX).
- **svidmint** issues the publisher workload identity (a SPIFFE JWT-SVID for the release job).
- **assayward** evaluates both against the `forgeseal-dogfood` policy and lets the release proceed only on `allow`.

The committed fixtures under `testdata/dogfood/` were generated by **actually running** forgeseal and svidmint (not synthesized), and `internal/forgeseal` evaluates them to `allow` (see `internal/forgeseal/dogfood_test.go`). The policy is `testdata/dogfood/policy-dogfood.yaml`.

## What is verified today vs. deferred

The dogfood proves the loop for the stages assayward can verify from real artifacts:

| Stage | Status |
|-------|--------|
| SLSA provenance (builder + subject-digest match) | verified |
| SBOM presence (CycloneDX) | verified |
| VEX (no unmitigated affected) | verified |
| Workload identity (svidmint JWT-SVID, trust domain + binding) | verified, cryptographically, via go-spiffe |
| **Sigstore image signature** | **deferred** — see below |

**Why signature is `required: false` in the dogfood policy:** forgeseal's Sigstore signing is currently a stub — it emits a bundle with a bare `dsseEnvelope` and an ECDSA signature but **no Fulcio certificate and no Rekor transparency-log entry** (`verificationMaterial` is absent). assayward's `sigstore-go` verifier requires the full keyless chain, so it cannot verify forgeseal's stub bundle. The loop therefore relies on SLSA provenance + the svidmint identity rather than the image signature until forgeseal completes real keyless signing. This is the one open piece of the §6.6 "signed with Sigstore, verified by forgeseal" loop.

## Wiring into the sibling repos

`forgeseal-release-gate.yml` and `svidmint-release-gate.yml` are the workflows that forgeseal/svidmint would add under their own `.github/workflows/` to gate their releases. They are provided here as the canonical reference; copying them into the sibling repos and enabling them is the cross-repo integration step.
